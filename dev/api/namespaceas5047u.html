◆ kContinuousWord

constexpr bool as5047u::kContinuousWord = false
inlineconstexpr

false: the chip counts clocks while CS is low and executes the frame at the rising CS edge, so idle CLK time between the bytes of one frame is allowed. CS must not rise inside a frame.

◆ kMinCsHighNs

constexpr uint32_t as5047u::kMinCsHighNs = 350
inlineconstexpr

Shortest time CS stays high between two frames, t_CSn (2 t_CLK_SYS + 10 ns with the internal clock at its 9 MHz typical; give margin on a slow internal clock).
