Hardware¶

The AS5047U's pins, bus settings, frames, timing, power and magnet, as the driver uses them.

All of them, with the driver API for each: Datasheet map.

Every value on this page comes from the AS5047U datasheet (ams OSRAM DS000637, v1-01); figure numbers refer to it. The driver is not verified on hardware yet: the values are the datasheet's, and the driver's behaviour against them is tested on the register model only.

The chip has no reset pin and no interrupt pin, so the port has no GPIO hook. Digital inputs must never float. The ABI, UVW and PWM outputs drive at most 4 mA at 5 V and 2 mA at 3.3 V, into at most 50 pF.

Bus settings¶

Setting Value
SPI mode 1: CPOL = 0, CPHA = 1. The chip samples MOSI on the falling edge of CLK. Modes 0, 2 and 3 do not work
Clock Up to 10 MHz (t_clk ≥ 100 ns)
Bit order MSB first
Chip select Active low, one frame per low period. CSn must go high between frames: the chip finds the frame length from the CSn low time and executes the frame on the rising edge
Reply The reply to a frame arrives during the next frame
Driver default 24-bit frames with CRC-8 (FrameFormat::Bits24)

Frames¶

"SPI Transaction" section, Figures 20 to 28. RW is 1 for a read and 0 for a write.

Format MOSI (host to chip) MISO (chip to host) Driver
16 bit [14] RW = 1, [13:0] address [15] warning, [14] error, [13:0] data Bits16: reads only, no CRC
24 bit [22] RW, [21:8] address or data, [7:0] CRC [23] warning, [22] error, [21:8] data, [7:0] CRC Bits24: the default
32 bit [31:24] pad, then the 24-bit frame The 24-bit frame, then [7:0] pad Bits32: daisy chains, pad from Config::pad_byte
Figure description and sources

Each frame is drawn in the order it is clocked, most significant bit first, and the numbers above a row are bit positions in that frame. On MOSI the 16-bit command word holds the R/W bit (1 to read, 0 to write) and a 14-bit address; the data frame of a write has the same layout with R/W at 0 and the 14-bit value in place of the address. On MISO the reply word holds the warning bit, the error bit (a flag is set in ERRFL) and 14 data bits. The 24-bit frame adds the CRC-8 of the word in both directions. The 32-bit frame adds the pad byte: first on MOSI (the driver sends Config::pad_byte), last on MISO (the driver ignores it). The bit positions come from the frame constants in inc/as5047u_registers.hpp and the byte order from detail::FrameBus::Exchange in inc/as5047u_spi_interface.hpp. With 16-bit frames the driver sends writes as 24-bit frames.

Drawn from: inc/as5047u_registers.hpp frame bits; inc/as5047u_spi_interface.hpp detail::FrameBus::Exchange; inc/as5047u_spi_interface.hpp Crc8; inc/as5047u.hpp writeFormat; figure module docs/diagrams/figures/spi-frames.py.

The datasheet allows 16-bit frames for reads only, so with Bits16 the driver sends every write as a 24-bit frame. Writes are always CRC-protected, and ProgramOtp() sends every frame, reads included, as a 24-bit frame whatever Config::frame says, then restores the format.

The CRC is CRC-8 with polynomial 0x1D, initial value 0xC4 and final XOR 0xFF, over the 16-bit word ("CRC Checksum"); as5047u::Crc8() computes it. A MOSI frame with a wrong CRC is not executed and sets ERRFL.CRC_error. A MISO frame with a wrong CRC fails the call with Error::Crc.

Read and write sequences¶

A read is two frames: the read command, then a NOP whose reply carries the data. When the reply's error bit is set, the driver reads ERRFL (two more frames, and the read clears it on the chip) to find out why.

sequenceDiagram
    participant D as Driver
    participant C as AS5047U
    D->>C: read ANGLECOM (0x3FFF)
    C-->>D: reply to the previous frame (discarded)
    D->>C: read NOP (0x4000)
    C-->>D: ANGLECOM data, warning and error bits, CRC
    Note over D: error bit set? read ERRFL and fail the call

A write is two frames, the command (RW = 0, address) and the data. The reply to the data frame reports on the command frame. With Verify::ReadBack, and always in EnsureInitialized() and ProgramOtp(), each write is then read back (two more frames) and compared. Brackets give the count with Verify::ReadBack.

ReadNonVolatile() 14 33.6 µs
ApplyConfig(), SetZeroPosition(), SetDirection() 26 [38] 62.4 µs [91.2 µs]

The times are derived (3 bytes of clock per frame), not measured. Add at least 350 ns of CSn high time per frame and your port's own overhead. A reply with the error bit set adds 2 frames for ERRFL.

Timing¶

Figure 19. The two 350 ns limits are the ones a port can break: CSn low to the first clock edge, and CSn high between two frames. A hardware chip select usually meets them; a GPIO chip select toggled right next to the transfer may not.

Symbol Parameter Min Max Unit
t_L CSn falling edge to first CLK rising edge 350 ns
t_clk Clock period 100 ns
t_clkH, t_clkL Clock high and low time 50 ns
t_H Last CLK falling edge to CSn rising edge t_clk / 2 ns
t_CSn CSn high time between frames 350 ns
t_MOSI MOSI valid before the clock edge 20 ns
t_MISO Clock edge to MISO valid 51 ns
t_OZ CSn rising edge to MISO high impedance 10 ns
t_pon VDD above its minimum to the first valid angle 10 ms

EnsureInitialized() covers t_pon itself: it polls DIA once per millisecond until every loop reports finished, for at most Config::startup_timeout_ms (default 10 ms, t_pon). The deadline counts from the first poll, so raise it if your supply ramps slowly or the start comes before VDD is up. The only other wait is in ProgramOtp(), 1 ms between polls of the burn. Both go through the port's DelayUs().

Power¶

Mode Supply pins Temperature Angle noise (rms)
5 V VDD 4.5 V to 5.5 V; VDD3V3 is the LDO output, 1 µF to GND -40 °C to 150 °C 0.068°
3.3 V, NOISESET = 0 VDD and VDD3V3 tied, 3.0 V to 3.6 V -40 °C to 125 °C 0.068°
3.3 V, NOISESET = 1 VDD and VDD3V3 tied, 3.0 V to 3.6 V -40 °C to 150 °C 0.082°
  • Config::high_temperature_noise sets NOISESET, for 150 °C operation at 3.3 V.
  • Supply current is at most 16 mA. Put 100 nF from VDD to GND in both modes.
  • Logic levels follow VDD: V_IH ≥ 0.7 × VDD, V_IL ≤ 0.3 × VDD. In 5 V mode a 3.3 V MCU needs a level shifter on CLK, MOSI and CSn (0.7 × 4.5 V = 3.15 V), and MISO swings up to VDD.
  • Absolute maximum: 7.0 V on VDD, 5.0 V on VDD3V3.
  • The chip has no low-power mode: Deinitialize() does no I/O.
  • ReadDiagnostics().value.vdd_5v is true in 5 V mode.
  • Burning the OTP has its own supply and temperature limits: OTP programming.

Magnet¶

Parameter Value
Type Diametrically magnetised (two poles across the diameter), centred on the package axis
Field at the die 35 mT to 70 mT, orthogonal component, along a circle of 1.1 mm radius
Datasheet reference magnet N35H, 8 mm diameter, 3 mm thick
Speed Up to 28000 rpm
Accuracy (optimal placement) ±0.8° at 25 °C, ±1° over -40 °C to 150 °C (maximum non-linearity)

The AGC keeps the field magnitude constant while the field is in range. ReadAgc() near 0 means the field is too strong (magnet too close), near 255 too weak (too far); ERRFL.AGC_warning is set at either limit. Read ERRFL or AGC regularly: the datasheet requires one of them to be read for the AGC loop to work as specified.

Outputs other than SPI¶

Output Pins What it carries Config fields
ABI A, B, I/PWM Quadrature, 25 to 4096 pulses per revolution; index once per revolution, 3 LSB or 1 LSB wide incremental, abi_enable, abi_resolution_code, abi_decimal, index_width, hysteresis_code
UVW U, V, W/PWM Hall-sensor emulation for 1 to 7 pole pairs incremental, uvw_enable, uvw_pole_pairs
PWM W/PWM or I/PWM 12-bit angle in a frame of 4119 PWM clocks of 444 ns typical (about 1.83 ms) pwm_enable

The PWM goes on pin W when ABI is selected and on pin I when UVW is selected (SETTINGS2.UVW_ABI). Config::uncompensated_at_angle_register (SETTINGS2.Data_select) makes address 0x3FFF and the PWM output carry the angle without DAEC. Configuration maps each field to its register.
